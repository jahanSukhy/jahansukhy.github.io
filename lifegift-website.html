Why It Matters

Every drop is a
gift of life

Blood cannot be manufactured — it can only come from generous people like you.

🚑

Emergency Surgeries

Accident victims and trauma patients often need large amounts of blood immediately. Your donation keeps emergency rooms prepared.

👶

Premature Babies

Newborns with severe health conditions often require multiple transfusions just to survive their first weeks of life.

🎗️

Cancer Treatment

Patients undergoing chemotherapy frequently need blood and platelet transfusions to manage treatment side effects.

Chronic Conditions

People with sickle cell disease, thalassemia, and other conditions rely on regular transfusions throughout their lives.

🌍

Global Impact

Your donation contributes to our worldwide network, helping regions facing natural disasters or healthcare shortages.

🔬

Medical Research

Donated blood also supports breakthrough research into blood disorders and transfusion medicine around the world.

📲 How notifications work

✅ Eligibility checklist

Make sure you meet these criteria before donating:

  • ✔ Age 18–65 years old
  • ✔ Weight at least 45 kg
  • ✔ Generally in good health
  • ✔ Haemoglobin ≥ 12.5 g/dL (women) / 13 g/dL (men)
  • ✔ At least 90 days since last donation
  • ✖ Not pregnant or recently gave birth
  • ✖ No recent tattoo or piercing (within 6 months)
  • ✖ Not on blood-thinning medication

🏛️ Our Story

The people behind
LifeGift

Founded in 1988 by a group of doctors and philanthropists who believed every life is worth saving — meet the team and the generous supporters who make it possible.

Who we are

Saving lives since 1988

LifeGift was born from a single belief: that no one should die for lack of blood. Today we operate in 30+ countries and have saved over 120,000 lives.

🩸

Our Mission

To make safe blood available to every person who needs it, regardless of location, wealth, or circumstance.

🌍

Our Vision

A world where no patient dies waiting for blood — where every eligible donor is connected to someone in need.

🤝

Our Values

Compassion, transparency, community. Every donor, every volunteer, every dollar is treated with deep gratitude.

Our journey

35 years of impact

From a single blood drive in Dhaka to a global network — here is how LifeGift grew.

1988
Founded in Dhaka, Bangladesh
Dr. Amir Hassan and five colleagues held the first LifeGift blood drive at Dhaka Medical College, collecting 48 units of blood in a single afternoon.
1993
First permanent blood bank opened
With funding from the Hassan Foundation, LifeGift opened its first permanent blood bank in Gulshan, Dhaka — capable of storing 2,000 units.
2001
Expanded to 5 countries
Operations spread across Pakistan, India, Sri Lanka, and Nepal. The international donor network reached 10,000 registered donors.
2010
Digital donor tracking launched
LifeGift introduced the first digital donor management system in South Asia, automating 90-day cycle tracking and reminder calls.
2018
30th anniversary — 30 countries
To mark 30 years, LifeGift expanded to its 30th country and surpassed 50,000 lives saved. The Hassan Scholarship Fund was established.
2024
SMS & WhatsApp notification system
Launched real-time SMS and WhatsApp reminders for donors — reducing the gap between eligible donors and blood requests by 67%.

1 Who we are

LifeGift Blood Bank ("LifeGift", "we", "us", or "our") is a nonprofit blood donation organisation founded in 1988 and operating in 30+ countries. Our registered address is 123 Health Street, Dhaka, Bangladesh.

We operate the website at lifegift.org and the LifeGift donor management platform, including the donor tracker, emergency board, and self-registration system.

For the purposes of data protection law, LifeGift is the data controller of your personal information.

Our commitment: We collect only what we need, keep it only as long as necessary, and never sell your personal data to anyone. Your data exists only to help connect blood donors with people who need it.

We do not collect payment card numbers, national ID numbers, or passwords. We do not require you to create an account to register as a blood donor.

3 How we use your data

We use your personal information for the following purposes:

  • Managing your donor record — tracking your 90-day donation cycle and donation history so we can send you accurate reminders.
  • Sending donation reminders — contacting you via SMS and/or WhatsApp when your 90-day waiting period is complete and you are eligible to donate again.
  • Emergency blood matching — when an emergency blood request is posted that matches your blood type and you are near or at eligibility, we may alert you directly.
  • Improving our service — analysing aggregate (non-personal) data to understand donation patterns and improve the platform.
  • Legal compliance — retaining records as required by health regulations and nonprofit law in the jurisdictions where we operate.
  • Honour board display — if you make a financial donation and consent to public display, your name and donation amount appear on our public donors page.

Legal basis (GDPR): We process your data under Article 6(1)(a) — consent (you gave it when registering), and Article 9(2)(c) — vital interests (blood donation is a matter of life and death). You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us.

4 SMS & WhatsApp notifications

📱 Notification consent

When you register as a donor you will be asked which types of notifications you want to receive:

  • 90-day reminder SMS — sent automatically when your 90-day donation cycle completes.
  • 90-day reminder WhatsApp — same as above via WhatsApp.
  • Emergency alerts — sent when a patient urgently needs blood matching your type.

Messages are sent via Twilio, a third-party communications platform. Your phone number is transmitted to Twilio solely for the purpose of sending these messages. Twilio does not use your number for any other purpose. See Twilio's privacy policy at twilio.com/legal/privacy.

Every message we send includes the instruction: "Reply STOP to unsubscribe." Replying STOP will immediately remove you from all future notifications. You can also unsubscribe by contacting us directly.

We will never send promotional, advertising, or fundraising messages to your phone without your explicit separate consent.

5 Data sharing & third parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Twilio — your phone number is shared to send SMS and WhatsApp notifications (see Section 4).
  • Partner hospitals — in a life-threatening emergency, limited contact information (name and phone number) may be shared with a partner hospital to facilitate an urgent blood donation. We will always tell you if this happens.
  • Legal obligation — we may disclose your data to a government body or law enforcement agency if required to do so by law.
  • Organisation succession — if LifeGift merges with or transfers operations to another nonprofit, your data will be transferred only if the receiving organisation agrees in writing to the same data protection standards as set out in this policy.

We will never share your health data (blood type, donation history) with insurance companies, employers, or commercial organisations.

7 Your rights

Under GDPR and applicable data protection laws, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access — you can request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — if any information is incorrect, you can ask us to correct it.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — you can ask us to delete your personal data. We will do so unless we are legally required to retain it.
  • Right to restrict processing — you can ask us to pause using your data while you raise a concern.
  • Right to data portability — you can request your data in a machine-readable format (CSV or JSON).
  • Right to object — you can object to us processing your data for notification purposes at any time.
  • Right to withdraw consent — you can withdraw consent at any time by replying STOP to any message or contacting us.

To exercise any of these rights, please email privacy@lifegift.org. We will respond within 30 days. There is no charge for making a request.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you have the right to complain to your national data protection authority.

8 Cookies

Our website uses the following types of cookies:

  • Essential cookies — required for the website to function (e.g. keeping your session active). These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors use the site (e.g. which pages are most visited). No personal data is shared with third parties through analytics.
  • Preference cookies — remember your choices (e.g. language settings).

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling non-essential cookies will not affect your ability to use the donor registration or emergency features.

9 Children's privacy

LifeGift's services are intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under the age of 18. Our donor registration form requires a date of birth and will not accept registrations from people under 18.

If you believe we have inadvertently collected data from a person under 18, please contact us immediately at privacy@lifegift.org and we will delete it without delay.

10 Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. When we make significant changes, we will:

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
  • Send a notification to all registered donors via SMS or WhatsApp at least 14 days before the changes take effect.
  • Post a prominent notice on our website homepage for 30 days.

Your continued use of our services after the effective date of any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. If you do not agree with the changes, you can withdraw your registration by contacting us.
